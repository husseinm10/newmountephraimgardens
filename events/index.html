Mount Ephraim Events

We have a full programme of events planned every season including family days, a classic car show and farming events organised by Mount Ephraim. We also have a number of events organised by third parties including outdoor theatre productions, a music festival, day retreats and a 10k run. The cost to attend our Mount Ephraim organised events is just our normal garden entry fee and ticket sales are usually released 6-8 weeks before each event and will be on sale via this page. Season ticket holders can come for free to events organised within the garden season, but must still register to attend so we can monitor capacity. We don’t offer discounted entry for HH Members or any 2 for 1 offers on our event days. For tickets to the outdoor theatre, retreat days, the 10k run and A New Day Festival please purchase tickets directly from the organisers. Event Terms and Conditions can be seen here.
