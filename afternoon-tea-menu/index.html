Please note: This is a sample menu and it can change slightly from time to time. We offer Afternoon Teas on Thursdays to Sundays between 12pm – 3pm during our garden season (April to September).

Afternoon Teas must be pre-booked and we require at least 48 hours notice, bearing in mind we are not open on Mondays and Tuesdays so can’t take bookings on these days.

All dietary requirements must be provided at the time of booking. We also have a Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan and Children’s Afternoon Tea Menu – available on request.
