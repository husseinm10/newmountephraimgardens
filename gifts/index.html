Mount Ephraim / Gifts

Treat a loved one

Why not purchase a guide book, afternoon tea gift voucher or season ticket for a loved one?

Everything in our online shop can be bought onsite from our ticket box during opening hours and online purchases are designed more as gift purchases. However if you prefer to purchase your season tickets online in advance of your first visit, you are very welcome to and we will post it out to you. If buying anything online please allow up to 5 working days to receive your items and please note our online price includes postage and packaging. Our site doesn’t send automatic confirmation of your order but we will endeavour to send you a manual confirmation by email within 48 hours of your order, so you know your order is on its way.
