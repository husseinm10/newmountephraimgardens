Mount Ephraim / Things to do locally

Things to do locally

In our charming village of Hernhill and within walking distance of Mount Ephraim

Treat yourself to Afternoon Tea or a Traditional Cream Tea in the Mount Ephraim Tea Room. Open April to September, Wednesdays to Sundays.

Dine at the Red Lion, Hernhill – the quintessential pub on the village green!

Enjoy a pint, or two, of traditional Kentish ales at the Three Horseshoes Pub.

Highlights of Faversham

Enjoy Faversham’s hidden gem at Standard Quay, ME13 7BS.

Waterside attractions, traditional barges, historic trail, Secret Garden Tearoom, Papa Bianco’s Pizza restaurant, Baltic House Wine Bar. Butchers, fishmongers, unique shops and outlets.

Visit Macknade – the South East’s leading food hall, delicatessen, butchery and cafe serving breakfast and lunch.

Experience the Sea-Scrub Sauna at Macknade – a new Nordic wood-fired barrel sauna, hot-tub and cold plunge barrels set in the tranquil countryside with glorious views.

Enjoy a brewery tour at Shepherd Neame, Britain’s oldest brewer.

Explore the beautiful gardens at Belmont House & Gardens or a guided tour of the historic house.

Go for an indoor or outdoor dip at Faversham Pools.

Relax with micro, miniature or just plain cute free range animals around their orchard home at Huckleberry Woods.
