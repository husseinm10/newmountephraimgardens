Mount Ephraim / Family Season Ticket 2027

Family Season Ticket

£65 – The family ticket is for two adults and two children aged 4 – 16 years. Plus £1.50 for P&P when purchased online.

Our season tickets are valid during normal opening hours (Wednesdays to Sundays and Bank Holidays between 11am and 4pm) from April to September 2027 with the exception of 18-24 August 2027 when we will be closed for A New Day Festival.

Season Ticket holders get free entry to our Mount Ephraim organised events including the Easter Trail, Lambing Weekend, Wine and Roses, Cherry Day, Classic Cars and Apple Sunday but you must register in advance to attend. Season Tickets do not include entry to any third party organised special events including festivals and theatre productions, or any out of season events.

Season ticket holders will receive a 10% discount on all purchases in the Tea Room, excluding any special offers.
