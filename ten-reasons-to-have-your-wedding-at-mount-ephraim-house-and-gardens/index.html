Why Choose Mount Ephraim House & Garden for Your Wedding

 1. Mount Ephraim is a privately-owned home

It’s not often that you can enjoy a beautiful setting in over 10 acres of garden and a lake – the luxury of someone else’s grand home with its sweeping staircase and feel like it’s yours for the day. The Dawes family will welcome you and make you feel totally at ease. They enjoy sharing their home with you, ensuring you have the very best day possible.

2. Luxurious onsite accommodation that can sleep up to 22

Weddings often mean some friends and family have to travel from far away and we know you want to make their life as easy and comfortable as possible. That’s why at Mount Ephraim we include accommodation for your guests as part of our package.

3. A wedding coordinator

If you have your wedding celebration at Mount Ephraim you will have exclusive access to our amazing wedding coordinator, who on the day will take care of all the details, supplier liaising and organisation, so you can totally relax.

4. Plenty of time to prepare

We know how much planning and time it takes to create your perfect day. At Mount Ephraim we never book back to back weddings, so you can have all the time you need.
You will have access to the venue the day before your big day and the day after too.

5. Food glorious food

Feeding your guests and delivering exceptional food and service is very important. We work exclusively with Scott Anderson who are award winning Kent caterers. They will discuss menu choices and create your desired wedding feast.

6. Mount Ephraim is very personable

Mount Ephraim House and Gardens is a perfect venue for small intimate celebrations or big parties. It’s up to you. You may decide to marry in the gardens in our Wedding Pavilion or inside the house. You can even make your mind up a few days before and wait on the weather – we can adapt to suit you.

7. Room for a marquee

We know lots of couples have dreams of a big marquee and finding a venue that can accommodate this can be tricky. Our large front lawn makes a wonderful place to have a marquee or chill-out yurt. We can cater for 200+.

8. Free parking

When your guests arrive, they will be able to park easily onsite and it’s totally free. It’s not often you go to a wedding and don’t have to worry about parking.

9. Location, location, location

Mount Ephraim House and Gardens is minutes from the M2 and ideally situated close to Faversham with its excellent rail links to London and beyond. Both Canterbury and Whitstable are only ten minutes away.

10. Exclusively unique

Because Mount Ephraim is a private home that is steeped in hundreds of years of history it is a very special place that we have the pleasure of sharing to a limited number of couples each year.

If you would like to find out more – why not book a visit and discover the Mount Ephraim magic for yourself, email: wedddings@mountephraimgardens.co.uk
