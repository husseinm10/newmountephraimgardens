A Warm Welcome

Mount Ephraim Country House & Gardens is a privately-owned family home in the heart of Kent, comprising over 800 acres of woodland, pasture, and a fruit farm. At the heart of the estate lies the main house and gardens with stunning views over the surrounding countryside.

Situated between Canterbury and Faversham and just an hour from London, Mount Ephraim Gardens boasts ten acres of Edwardian gardens open to the public between April and September, luxury accommodation including a self catering holiday let sleeping 12, five ensuite guest rooms, and two glamping pods. The beautiful manor house in Kent is also available to hire for weddings, celebrations and private events.
