Mount Ephraim / Private Events

Private Events

Hire Mount Ephraim for your private event or celebration.

At Mount Ephraim Gardens, we offer a beautiful and flexible setting for life’s meaningful moments. Set within a historic Edwardian estate surrounded by ten acres of landscaped gardens, our light-filled reception rooms and peaceful outdoor spaces provide an elegant backdrop for celebrations of life, baby showers, birthdays, anniversaries, private dinners, and family gatherings. Whether you’re hosting a small occasion or a larger event, our rooms can be styled to suit the atmosphere you have in mind.

Our reception rooms can be used as one large space for up to 100 guests or divided into two smaller rooms, seating 65 and 35 respectively, offering the perfect setting for both larger and more intimate gatherings. For very private occasions, our private dining room seats up to 12 guests. The adjacent hall is ideal for drinks receptions and dancing, while our boutique bar provides a relaxed spot for cocktails and evening socialising.

We know every event is personal, and our team will work with you from the outset to ensure everything runs smoothly. With flexible hire options, trusted local catering partners, and a choice of indoor and outdoor spaces, hosting your occasion at Mount Ephraim Gardens is simple and seamless. If you’re looking for a distinctive private event venue in Kent, our estate offers the charm, privacy, and warm hospitality to make your gathering truly memorable.

Our beautiful on-site accommodation can also be included as part of your event package, allowing you and your guests to stay overnight and enjoy the estate to the fullest.

Please contact lucy@mountephraimgardens.co.uk for more information.
