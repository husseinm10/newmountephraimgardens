Kent Wedding Venues

UNIQUE COUNTRY HOUSE WEDDING VENUE

NEAR FAVERSHAM AND CANTERBURY

Mount Ephraim is the ideal choice for your wedding venue, offering a unique blend of elegance and natural beauty. Located in the picturesque Kent countryside, our venue provides the perfect ceremony and reception spaces that can be adjusted to fit your needs, whether you prefer to exchange your vows indoors or outdoors. 

Kent offers many wedding venues, but none match the charm and flexibility of Mount Ephraim. With exclusive use of our manor house, a personalised service, award-winning caterers and stunning views, your wedding day will be unforgettable.

AT MOUNT EPHRAIM WE OFFER:

UNIQUE VENUE

Mount Ephraim Country House and Gardens is a privately owned family home offering a unique, classically romantic venue for your wedding. From the spectacular views of the gardens and estate to the elegant sweeping staircase leading into the main hall, we have the perfect venue to make your big day extra special.

CEREMONIES

We can accommodate ceremonies of up to 100 people in the main house or 150 in our Edwardian Garden. With beautiful staircases both in the main house and gardens, we offer a grand entrance.

HOUSE RECEPTIONS

For a reception in the house, we can host wedding breakfasts for up to 100 in our beautiful dining room. And for your evening party we can accommodate up to 175 guests with a boutique bar and fabulous dancefloor to dance the night away.

MARQUEE RECEPTIONS

For a marquee reception in our ten acres of gardens we can accommodate over 250 guests and up to 150 for a ceremony, for that perfect garden wedding.

ONSITE ACCOMMODATION

We offer accommodation for up to 22 guests the night before and night of your wedding day, including a spectacular Wedding Suite, four beautiful ensuite guest rooms and the Garden Wing shared accommodation sleeping 12, so you and your special guests can wake up to the stunning views and setting and enjoy more time together.

AWARD WINNING CATERING

We are proud to work with our exclusive catering partners, Scott Anderson Ltd, winners of Best Caterer in the National Wedding Awards 2026. Their friendly and professional team will help curate exceptional food and drink for your guests, ensuring your wedding catering is an unforgettable part of your day.

Ten reasons to have your wedding day at Mount Ephraim

LOCATION

Only an hour away from London, we are a very short drive from Canterbury, Faversham, Sittingbourne, and Whitstable and offer something unlike anything else in the area.

INSIDE OR OUT

Our stunning location offers both indoor and outdoor wedding locations, making Mount Ephraim one of the perfect wedding venues for any season or weather conditions. Whether you prefer an intimate indoor ceremony or a grand outdoor celebration, our estate provides the ideal backdrop for your special day.

THE WAY YOU WANT IT

Our team is dedicated to ensuring every detail is tailored to your vision, creating an unforgettable experience for you and your guests. We know it’s difficult to plan a wedding for the British weather but with the country house available as well as the extensive gardens, we’re about as weather-proof as you can find. Plus, we can help recommend florists, marquees, music, and everything else to help make your day truly special. View Our Recommended Suppliers.

TIME TO ENJOY IT

You’ll have exclusive use of the house on your wedding day and we will allow you access to the house the day before and the day after for a less rushed, more intimate experience. At Mount Ephraim, you can relax, take your time and enjoy your moment.
