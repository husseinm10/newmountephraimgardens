Glamping Pods Kent | Mount Ephraim

Tucked away on the edge of our gardens, overlooking the fruit farm, our two glamping pods offer a comfortable and relaxed way to enjoy the Mount Ephraim estate.

Each pod sleeps up to four, with a comfortable double bed, sofa bed, kitchenette and ensuite bathroom. Outside, there’s a private deck with seating, a hammock and firepit – everything you need for a slower pace of life and a few days in the countryside. Dogs are welcome too.

Spend your days exploring the gardens, walking through the surrounding countryside or heading out to discover Whitstable, Canterbury and Faversham. Back at Mount Ephraim, you can enjoy the peace of the estate and access to the gardens outside public opening hours.

Open all year round, our pods are ideal for couples, families and anyone looking for a countryside break with a little something different.
