Group Retreats at Mount Ephraim

Wellness • Nature • Escape

Looking for somewhere special to bring your group together for a relaxing getaway?

Our accommodation at Mount Ephraim offers a unique setting for small group stays, surrounded by our beautiful gardens and countryside. Whether you’re planning a wellness weekend, a girls’ getaway, a birthday celebration or simply some time away with friends, you can make your stay your own.

Wellness experiences can be arranged to complement your stay, with a selection of local practitioners and experiences available to book for your group, including:

  • Private yoga/pilates sessions – a relaxing and restorative class tailored to your group
  • Horsebox Sauna – enjoy a private sauna experience at Mount Ephraim
  • Reflexology – relaxing treatments brought to you at the estate
  • Other wellness treatments – we can help you arrange additional experiences depending on your group and availability

Stay in our Garden Wing, sleeping up to 12 guests, or our West Wing, with five ensuite bedrooms, and enjoy having Mount Ephraim as your base for a memorable group getaway.

Our wellness experiences are arranged separately from your accommodation, giving you the flexibility to create a weekend that suits your group.

Interested in planning a group stay? Get in touch with us to discuss your dates, accommodation requirements and the wellness experiences you’d like to include.

For more information, please email lucy@mountephraimgardens.co.uk
