Mount Ephraim / Stay with us

Escape to the countryside

Relax in True Country House Style

Escape to the Kent countryside and stay in one of Mount Ephraim’s unique accommodation options. All of our stays combine charm, comfort and individuality, with beautiful views across the estate and exclusive access to our ten acres of landscaped gardens throughout your visit.

Whether you’re looking for a self-catering country house stay in the Garden Wing, an exclusive weekend stay in the West Wing, or a rural retreat in one of our glamping pods, Mount Ephraim offers the perfect base to relax, unwind and explore Kent. Be one of the first to try our new group Retreats to help you unwind, reconnect and focus on your wellbeing.

The Garden Wing – Spacious Self-Catering Accommodation sleeping 12

Perfect for larger groups, family gatherings or weekends away with friends, the Garden Wing is a self-catering option with plenty of space to relax and socialise. With six double bedrooms, two shared bathrooms and a downstairs loo, along with generous downstairs living areas including large kitchen/diner, two sitting rooms and a games room, this beautiful wing offers a real country house experience.

The West Wing – private weekend stays

For a more intimate, exclusive stay, the West Wing features five elegant double ensuite guest rooms, each individually styled and set within the historic house. Ideal for couples or friends looking to unwind, the West Wing offers a peaceful and refined country house atmosphere.

Guest rooms are available to book individually on selected weekends and include access to the estate’s gardens and grounds during your stay.

Glamping Pods – Rural Retreats Overlooking the Fruit Farm

For something a little different, try our new glamping pods on the edge of the gardens. Overlooking our fruit farm, they’re the perfect blend of rustic charm and modern comfort — ideal for couples, young families or anyone seeking an outdoor rural experience in Kent.

Wake up to birdsong, enjoy peaceful views, and step straight into the gardens for your morning stroll.

Exclusive Hire of Mount Ephraim

For weddings, private events or celebrations, you can hire all of Mount Ephraim’s accommodation along with the main end of the house — a fabulous and flexible space for hosting unforgettable gatherings.

Explore Kent from Mount Ephraim

Located just an hour from London, Mount Ephraim is perfectly placed for exploring the best of Kent. We’re less than ten minutes from the historic market town of Faversham, the seaside charm of Whitstable, and the cultural heritage of Canterbury with its world-famous Cathedral.

From country walks and coastal escapes to vineyards, farm shops and award-winning pubs, there’s plenty to enjoy right on our doorstep.
