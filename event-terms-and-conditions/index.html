Our Events Terms & Conditions

  • Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable in all circumstances including poor weather. If for any reason we are unable to run the event, your tickets will be transferable to another event or to visit the gardens on an alternative day. In the unlikely event this is not possible then we will refund your tickets.
  • We will use all reasonable endeavours to ensure that all event activities run as planned. However, we reserve the right, where appropriate, whether for safety reasons, or otherwise, and with or without prior notice, to substitute or cancel any activity.
  • The event will run between 11am and 4pm with last entry at 3pm
  • There are areas of open water at Mount Ephraim and cars on site accessing the main drive and driveway around the house – children must be supervised at all times.
  • Well behaved dogs on leads are welcome to attend events. Please clear up after them.
  • All car parking is free but cars are parked at your own risk.
  • We ask all visitors to keep personal belongings with them. We accept no responsibility for the theft of any property or personal item whilst on our premises.
  • Any 2 for 1 offers and Historic Houses memberships are not valid on event days.
